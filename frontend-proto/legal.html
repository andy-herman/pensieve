About Pensieve

Pensieve is a free, unofficial personal project that adds a memory layer to your Microsoft To Do tasks. It copies your tasks to this PC, uses AI to note why each one matters and which of your goals it serves, and shows them on a board. It runs on your PC: there is no Pensieve account and no Pensieve server. Source code: github.com/andy-herman/pensieve.

Privacy

What stays on this PC. Stored for your Windows user only:

  • copies of your tasks and the AI-written notes about them, your goals, settings and model choices;
  • your Microsoft sign-in, encrypted with Windows data protection so only your Windows user can read it (your GitHub sign-in is kept by the GitHub Copilot CLI in Windows Credential Manager);
  • logs, which can include task titles when something goes wrong.

Pensieve collects no analytics or usage telemetry and sends nothing to its author. In the installed app this lives in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Pensieve; uninstalling offers to delete it. More > Accounts disconnects your sign-ins at any time.

The app's engine listens only on this PC (127.0.0.1) and answers only the Pensieve window it opened: each launch creates a new private key, so web pages and other people signed in to this PC cannot read your board.

What leaves this PC, and only when you use the feature:

  • Microsoft (Microsoft Graph). After you sign in, Pensieve reads your To Do lists and tasks, and adds a task only when you confirm one. Governed by your Microsoft account terms and the Microsoft Privacy Statement.
  • GitHub Copilot. Task titles, notes, list names and your goals are sent to GitHub Copilot to write summaries, drafts and recaps, using your own Copilot plan under GitHub's terms and privacy statement for that plan (Copilot may route requests to model providers GitHub works with). Pensieve runs each request with all tools disabled and deletes its Copilot session afterwards; GitHub's own retention rules still apply. You choose the models in More > AI models.
  • Speech. When you use Speak a task, your voice is processed by the speech recognition service built into Microsoft Edge. Nothing is recorded by Pensieve.
  • Optional vault bridge. If you turn it on, Pensieve writes a summary of your board into a folder you choose, and agents you run can ask it to add tasks (you approve each one). Any sync service you use copies that folder.

AI notice

AI-written summaries, goal matches, task drafts and recaps can be wrong or incomplete. Review them before relying on them. They are not professional, legal, financial, medical or career advice. You are responsible for any task you add and anything you send based on Pensieve's output. Nothing is added to your task lists without your confirmation.

Your responsibilities

Connect only accounts you own or are allowed to use. If you connect a work or school account, or put work information into Pensieve, follow your employer's or school's policies on AI tools and data. Keep Windows and your accounts secured: anyone who can sign in as your Windows user can open Pensieve.
